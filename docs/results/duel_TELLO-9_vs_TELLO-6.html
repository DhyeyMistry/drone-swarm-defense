Defense duel: TELLO-9 (defender) vs TELLO-6 (attacker)

Action Space hackathon, 24 Oct 2026 14:36 · real Tello flight, one defender against one attacker, our our_defense/ stack

Home test with the latest defend.py (instant takeoff, advance toward tag 10, aim at the current position). The defender re-attacks repeatedly, closing to 0.17-0.28 m several times between 25 s and 47 s. The attacker's camera shows it flipped at about 40 s, after which its video ends; its feed reported it down at 54 s.

Setup

  • Defender TELLO-9: defend.py. Localizes from tag 10 with its own camera (upright_positional.py), gets the attacker's position from the telemetry feed, rams it (defense_core.py).
  • Attacker TELLO-6: dummy_attacker.py. Takes off, holds facing tag 10 and shuttles 0.5 m toward / away from it, publishing its own position as repo fleet telemetry (the drone_gui format).
  • Started together by duel.py --defenders TELLO-9 --attackers TELLO-6 --fly (the attacker gets a short head start). Roles are command-line arguments.
  • All positions are in the tag-10 world frame of the map: x right of tag 10, y up from its centre, z out from the wall.

What happened

Grey bands: the defender had no tag fix (typically the attacker in front of it blocking the tag), so it held still instead of flying blind. Gaps in the line are those moments.

  1. 0–25.4 s: the defender rams from nan m away and enters the 0.35 m contact radius at 25.4 s.
  2. 25.4–54.0 s: it overshoots, turns back and passes again within 0.16 m. Grey bands (40% of the engagement) are moments without a tag fix, typically the attacker blocking the defender's view of the tag. It holds still each time instead of flying blind, as designed, then resumes.
  3. 54.0 s: TELLO-6's feed reports it down. The defender drops the target and switches to holding its guard position.

Attacker feed: received at 17 Hz on the defender side, median report age 120 ms while it was flying.

Every axis moved the commanded way. The drone keeps coasting about 2 s after a pulse, and rc 30 moves it about 2–3× farther than the controller assumes. That explains the overshoot in the duel above, and it is the next tuning item.

Problems found and fixed along the way

  • Dark room, tag on a screen: no tag detected and the Tello drifted into a wall. Fix: lights on, printed tag, and no flying blind: the drone lands if the tag is lost for 3–4 s (one timer per flight).
  • Upside-down / official-orientation tags: the repo localizer mirrors x and y with the official tag prints. Fix: upright_positional.py turns every tag upright before using the map, for either print style.
  • Climbing out of the camera's view: fix: defenders stay within ±0.5 m of the tag height, plus a 30 cm climb after takeoff so the tag comes into view.
  • Attacker feed convention: verified against drone_gui --sim's ground truth (0.12–0.18 m error). Attacker height now comes from the Tello's own height sensor (h).

Limitations and next steps

  • This report is one defender against one scripted attacker at short range (about 1 m).
  • The scripted attacker shuttles predictably; real attackers move more and faster.
  • The controller does not model the Tello's ~2 s coast after a command, so it overshoots and re-attacks. Next: braking from measured velocity, and a full-speed strike for the final metre.
  • Attacker positions are only as good as the feed, and every tag the attackers see must be in the map.
